# Team data checklist

No item below is supplied by the demo project. Record provenance, date, owner, coordinate frame, units, and uncertainty for every dataset.

## Geometry

- CAD-derived hardpoint XYZ coordinates
- vehicle origin and axis convention
- left/right and front/rear naming convention
- wishbone, tie-rod, upright, spring/damper, pushrod, rocker, and anti-roll-bar topology
- wheel centre, spin axis references, wheel/tire dimensions, and static ride height
- joint constraints, offsets, travel limits, and packaging envelopes

## Mass and force properties

- sprung/unsprung masses, corner weights, CG, and inertias
- spring rates, free lengths, installed lengths, preload, and bump stops
- damper force-velocity data with temperature/test conditions
- anti-roll-bar geometry and stiffness
- tire stiffness and force/moment data with test conditions

## Validation references

- CAD motion-study outputs at agreed travel/steering samples
- alignment measurements and tolerances
- rig measurements or damper/spring test reports
- telemetry channels with calibration and sample metadata
- identity/version of any reference solver and its settings

## Branding and access

- rights-cleared official logo and brand guidance
- approved handling rules for private vehicle data
- deployment accounts, retention policy, and authorized users
